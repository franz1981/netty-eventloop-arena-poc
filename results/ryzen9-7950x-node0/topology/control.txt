  w1-no  markers=false  serverCPU=26.50 s   250319.50 req/s
  w4-mk  markers=true   serverCPU=33.06 s   406 requests (client-limited)
  w4-no  markers=false  serverCPU= 1.06 s   384 requests (client-limited)

The IterationEnd tail task is always pending, so SingleThreadEventLoop.hasTasks() is always
true and the selector never blocks. On a saturated loop (W1) that costs +3.3% CPU and no
throughput. On a near-idle loop (W4) it costs 31x CPU and turns the iteration counter into a
spin counter: use the wall-clock column for W4, not the iteration column.
